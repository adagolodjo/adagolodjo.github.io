## Academic Background

I am **Dr. Yinoussa Adagolodjo**, an Assistant Professor at the University of Lille and a full member of the DEFROST team since 2019. My academic journey has been driven by a passion for bridging the gap between advanced robotics technology and practical healthcare applications.

### **Education**
- **PhD in Computer Science** - University of Strasbourg (2015-2019)
- **Master's in Robotics** - University of Lille (2013-2015)
- **Bachelor's in Computer Science** - University of Lille (2010-2013)

### **Current Position**
- **Assistant Professor** - Department of Computer Science, University of Lille
- **Research Team Member** - DEFROST Team, CRIStAL UMR 9189
- **Research Focus** - Robotics and Augmented Reality in Healthcare

## Research Philosophy

My research philosophy centers on the belief that technology should serve humanity, particularly in critical areas like healthcare. I focus on developing practical solutions that can be directly applied in clinical settings, ensuring that theoretical advances translate into real-world benefits.

### **Core Research Areas**

#### **Medical Robotics**
- Surgical assistance systems
- Robotic guidance for minimally invasive procedures
- Haptic feedback and force control
- Real-time surgical planning

#### **Augmented Reality in Healthcare**
- 3D visualization for surgical guidance
- Real-time overlay of medical imaging
- Interactive surgical planning interfaces
- Training and education applications

#### **Soft Robotics**
- Deformable object simulation
- Cosserat theory applications
- Control strategies for soft robots
- Medical applications of soft robotics

#### **Real-time Simulation**
- Finite element modeling
- GPU-accelerated computation
- Interactive deformation simulation
- Surgical workflow optimization

## Key Research Projects

### **CONECT Project (Doctoral Thesis)**
My doctoral research focused on integrating robotic technology within operating rooms to enhance precision in needle insertion procedures. This project involved:

- Developing finite element simulations for soft tissue modeling
- Creating augmented reality guidance systems
- Implementing real-time surgical assistance
- Validating solutions in clinical environments

### **ROBOCOP Project**
Current research on cochlear implant robotization, exploring innovative approaches to improve hearing technologies through:

- Advanced robotic control systems
- Precision surgical assistance
- Real-time monitoring and feedback
- Clinical validation studies

### **COSSEROOTS Project**
Applying Cosserat's theory to advance the control of slender deformation robots, focusing on:

- Theoretical framework development
- Control algorithm implementation
- Medical robotics applications
- Real-time simulation systems
