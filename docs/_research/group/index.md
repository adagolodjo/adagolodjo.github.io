## DEFROST Team Overview

The DEFROST (Deformable Robotic Softwares Team) is a cutting-edge research group at the University of Lille, focusing on developing innovative solutions in the field of deformable robotics and real-time simulation. The team is composed of interdisciplinary experts in robotics, computer science, and applied mathematics.

### Research Areas

- **Deformable Object Simulation**: Creating realistic simulations for deformable objects using soft robotics principles.
- **Soft Robotics**: Developing new algorithms and control techniques for soft, flexible robots.
- **Robotic Surgery and Medical Applications**: Enhancing surgical techniques and medical procedures through robotics.
- **Augmented Reality and Visualization**: Utilizing AR technologies to improve interaction with robotic systems and simulations.

### Key Projects

- **CONECT Project**: Real-time simulation and robotic assistance in medical procedures, focusing on needle insertion.
- **COSSEROOTS Project**: Applying Cosserat theory for advanced control of slender robots in surgical applications.
- **ROBOCOP Project**: Investigating cochlear implant robotization for improved auditory technologies.

### Facilities and Resources

The DEFROST team is equipped with state-of-the-art facilities, including:

- **Robotic Surgery Simulator**: A high-fidelity simulator for training and research into robotic-assisted surgical techniques.
- **Soft Robotics Lab**: A dedicated space for developing and testing flexible robot systems.
- **Visualization Suite**: Advanced tools for augmented reality and 3D visualization, enhancing interaction with research outcomes.

### Collaboration and Partnerships

The team maintains active collaborations with academic, industrial, and clinical partners worldwide. Interested parties are welcome to connect for joint research initiatives and technology transfer opportunities.

### Achievements and Recognition

- **Innovation in Technology Award** for contributions to robotic surgery and medical application development.
