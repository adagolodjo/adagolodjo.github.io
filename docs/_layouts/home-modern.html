About me

Robotics research at the frontier of medicine

I am an Assistant Professor at the University of Lille within the DEFROST team (Inria / CRIStAL UMR 9189). My work focuses on real-time deformable object simulation, needle insertion guidance, soft & continuum robotics, and augmented reality for surgical assistance. The goal: make minimally invasive procedures safer, more precise, and more accessible.
