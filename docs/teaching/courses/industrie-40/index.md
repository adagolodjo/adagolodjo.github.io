## Course Information

- **Course Code**: INDUSTRIE-40
- **Level**: Graduate
- **Semester**: Fall 2024
- **Hours**: 4.5h CM + 6h TP
- **Evaluation**: Exposé, Note de Classe

## Course Description

This course introduces students to the concepts and technologies driving the Fourth Industrial Revolution (Industry 4.0). Students will explore how cyber-physical systems, Internet of Things (IoT), artificial intelligence, and data analytics are transforming manufacturing and industrial processes.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this course, students will be able to:

- Understand the fundamental principles and pillars of Industry 4.0
- Analyze the impact of digital transformation on manufacturing processes
- Design and implement IoT solutions for industrial applications
- Evaluate the benefits and challenges of smart manufacturing systems
- Apply Industry 4.0 concepts to real-world industrial scenarios

## Topics Covered

### Lectures (CM - 4.5h)

#### Module 1: Introduction to Industry 4.0
- Historical context: From Industry 1.0 to 4.0
- Key technologies and enabling factors
- Digital transformation in manufacturing

#### Module 2: Cyber-Physical Systems (CPS)
- Architecture and components of CPS
- Integration of physical and digital systems
- Real-time monitoring and control

#### Module 3: Internet of Things (IoT) in Industry
- Industrial IoT (IIoT) fundamentals
- Sensor networks and data collection
- Communication protocols and standards

#### Module 4: Data Analytics and AI
- Big data in manufacturing
- Machine learning applications
- Predictive maintenance and quality control

### Practical Work (TP - 6h)

#### Lab 1: IoT Sensor Implementation
- Hands-on experience with industrial sensors
- Data acquisition and processing
- Communication protocols (MQTT, OPC-UA)

#### Lab 2: Smart Manufacturing Simulation
- Digital twin concepts
- Simulation of manufacturing processes
- Performance optimization using data analytics

#### Lab 3: Industry 4.0 Case Study
- Analysis of real industrial transformation projects
- Design of a smart factory concept
- Presentation of proposed solutions

## Assessment

- **Exposé (Presentation)**: Final project presentation on an Industry 4.0 implementation strategy
- **Note de Classe (Class Participation)**: Active participation in lectures and practical sessions
