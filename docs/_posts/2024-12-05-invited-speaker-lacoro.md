I'm excited to be invited as a speaker at **LACORO 2024** (Latin American Conference on Operations Research), taking place from **December 9-13, 2024** in Rancagua, Chile.

<!--more-->

## About LACORO

LACORO is the premier Latin American conference bringing together researchers, practitioners, and academics in operations research and related fields. The conference provides an excellent platform for sharing cutting-edge research and fostering collaboration across the Americas.

## Event Details

- **Dates**: December 9-13, 2024
- **Location**: Rancagua, Chile
- **Conference**: Latin American Conference on Operations Research
- **Duration**: 5 days of presentations, workshops, and networking

## Conference Highlights

LACORO 2024 features:
- **Registration Opens**: June 1, 2024
- **Early Registration Deadline**: October 1, 2024
- **Poster Submissions**: Open until September 15, 2024
- **Travel and Registration Grants**: Available with applications closing September 15, 2024

## Significance

This conference provides an important opportunity to connect with the Latin American operations research community and to share our latest work in computational methods and optimization techniques applied to robotics and medical applications.

Looking forward to the rich exchanges and collaborations that will emerge from this gathering of the Latin American scientific community!
