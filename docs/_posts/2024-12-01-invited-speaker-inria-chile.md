I'm honored to be invited as a speaker at the **Journées Scientifiques Inria Chile 2024**, taking place from **December 4-6, 2024** in Santiago, Chile at the Gabriela Mistral Cultural Center.

<!--more-->

## About the Event

This year's conference serves as an official pre-event of the AI Action Summit, consolidating Chile as a key player in the global discussion on artificial intelligence. The event brings together leading French and Chilean scientists to address the challenges and opportunities of AI today.

The Journées Scientifiques marks a key milestone in our path towards an AI-driven future and consolidates itself as a unique meeting space of the French-Chilean artificial intelligence ecosystem.

## Event Details

- **Dates**: December 4-6, 2024
- **Location**: Gabriela Mistral Cultural Center, Santiago, Chile
- **Language**: English
- **Format**: Three days of conferences, panel discussions, and networking

## Significance

As the only official pre-event in Chile for the AI Action Summit, this conference serves as a strategic platform to prepare the ground for the AI Action Summit in February in Paris. It's an excellent opportunity to contribute to the international dialogue on artificial intelligence and scientific computing.

Looking forward to sharing our latest research and connecting with the vibrant AI community in Chile!
