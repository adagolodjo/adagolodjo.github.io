I was honored to be invited as a speaker at the **CAIR Hong Kong Artificial Intelligence and Robotics in Healthcare Forum**, held from **March 28-29, 2024** in Hong Kong.

<!--more-->

## About the Event

The forum was organized by the Centre for Artificial Intelligence and Robotics (CAIR), Hong Kong Institute of Science & Technology, focusing on how artificial intelligence and robotics technologies are rapidly reshaping healthcare and medical practices.

## Event Focus

In this data-driven era, artificial intelligence and robotics technologies are having a profound impact in the field of medicine and healthcare, from image-assisted diagnosis to intelligent health management. AI is emerging as a pivotal force propelling medical progress.

## Event Details

- **Dates**: March 28-29, 2024
- **Location**: Hong Kong
- **Organizer**: Centre for Artificial Intelligence and Robotics (CAIR)
- **Focus**: AI and Robotics applications in Healthcare

This forum provided an excellent platform to discuss the latest developments in medical AI and robotics, and to share our research on robotic assistance and augmented reality for healthcare applications.
