## 🔬 Research Areas

```
Soft Robotics  ·  Medical Simulation  ·  Cosserat Rod Theory
Needle Insertion Robotics  ·  Augmented Reality in Surgery  ·  FEM Simulation
```

My research focuses on **real-time simulation and control of deformable robots and flexible medical instruments**, with applications in minimally invasive surgery, prostate biopsy guidance, and endoscopy.
